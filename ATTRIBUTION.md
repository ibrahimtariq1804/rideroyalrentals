## Modifications

The supplied converted GLB was recoloured with an obsidian-black PBR paint
treatment. Disconnected geometry was separated and regrouped into named hinge
nodes for four doors, the hood, and the powered rear hatch. Corrected local
pivots, metadata, and the `Open_All_Panels` verification animation were added.

Keep this attribution in the project repository and include a visible model
credit in the website footer, About page, or legal/credits page.
